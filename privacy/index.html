Privacy Policy – RF Survey Companion

Location data

RF Survey Companion may access the device's location when the user uses location-enabled RF survey features. Location information is used locally on the device to associate RF measurements with their geographical position. RF Survey Companion does not transmit location data to the developer or to third-party services.

Measurement data

RF measurements and related survey information are stored locally on the user's device. The application does not automatically upload measurement data to the developer or third-party services.

Error reports and sharing

RF Survey Companion does not automatically transmit crash reports or diagnostic information. Users may choose to share information using Android's system sharing functionality. Data shared in this way is sent only to the destination selected by the user.

Analytics and advertising

RF Survey Companion does not use analytics services, advertising services, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. RF Survey Companion does not collect or store payment-card information.
